Kalopaideia — Privacy Policy

Effective: May 27, 2026

Operator: New Charter Ventures LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("we," "us," "our," "New Charter Ventures").
Site: newcharterventures.com/paideia (the "Site"), together with related applications, APIs, audio streams, downloads, and services (collectively, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, retain, disclose, and protect personal information in connection with the Service. It is incorporated into our Terms of Service by reference. Defined terms used in our Terms of Service have the same meaning here unless otherwise stated.

If there is a conflict between this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service on a privacy-specific issue, this Privacy Policy controls unless the Terms expressly state otherwise.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information reasonably necessary to operate the Service.

Technical information collected automatically:

  • Server access logs, including IP address, user-agent string, and request timestamps.
  • Standard browser cookies used for session preferences and authentication state.

Information you provide when you create an account:

  • Name and email address.
  • A hashed password (we never see your password in cleartext).
  • Profile preferences.

Information generated by your use of the Service (if you are signed in):

  • Reading bookmarks and Commonplace entries.
  • Curriculum progress, including lesson completion status.
  • Capstone-exam responses and scores.
  • Diploma issuance records.
  • Purchase history.

Reading-analytics information (subject to consent — see Section 6).

We do not intentionally request special categories of personal data (such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, political opinions, health data, or biometric data) or sensitive personal information (as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act). Please do not submit such data through profile fields, Commonplace entries, contact forms, or other user-submitted areas of the Service. If you submit such information despite this request, we will process it only as necessary to provide the Service, respond to your request, comply with law, or delete it.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • provide the Service to you (account management, content delivery, audio streaming, capstone-exam scoring, Diploma issuance);
  • process subscription payments through Stripe and detect fraudulent transactions;
  • secure, audit, and improve the Site (including through aggregated, anonymized analytics);
  • respond to your inquiries, corrections requests, and legal notices;
  • comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations; and
  • enforce or defend our legal rights and the Terms of Service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not place advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts beyond those reasonably necessary to operate and audit the Site.

2.1 Lawful bases for processing (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we process personal data on the following lawful bases under Article 6(1) of the GDPR (and equivalent provisions of UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP):

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): to provide the Service to you, including account management, content delivery, subscription billing, and capstone-exam scoring.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): to operate, secure, audit, and improve the Site (including aggregate anonymized analytics), and to defend or enforce our legal rights.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for reading-analytics processing in the EEA/UK/CH (see Section 6) and for any optional marketing communications you may opt into.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): to comply with tax, accounting, and other legal obligations.

3. Service Providers and Sub-Processors

We share personal information with the following categories of service providers, each acting on our behalf under written contract:

  • Payment processing — Stripe, Inc. When you make a purchase, you provide your payment details directly to Stripe. We do not store your full payment-card number; we receive only a transaction reference, the amount charged, and a tokenized identifier. Stripe's privacy practices are governed by its own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Authentication — Supabase, Inc. Account sign-in is provided through Supabase Auth and may, at your election, include email-password authentication, email magic-link authentication, or OAuth sign-in through Google or another identity provider. Use of OAuth providers is also governed by those providers' privacy policies.
  • Synthetic-audio delivery — Microsoft Azure Speech and other speech-synthesis providers. Text strings sent for synthesis are processed by the provider for the sole purpose of returning the corresponding audio; we do not authorize providers to retain or repurpose your reading data.
  • Hosting and infrastructure — DigitalOcean, Hetzner, and similar cloud providers. Our application and database run on commercial cloud infrastructure located in the United States and, where applicable, in the European Economic Area.

We require each service provider to use personal information only to perform services for us and to maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use a minimal set of first-party browser cookies necessary to (a) keep you signed in, (b) remember session preferences (e.g., audio playback settings), and (c) maintain cross-site single sign-on between Kalopaideia and The Reading Mansion. Our cross-site sign-on token is scoped to the newcharterventures.com domain; you may sign out at any time, which invalidates the token on both sites.

We do not place advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral-analytics scripts.

5. Authentication and Cross-Site Sign-On

Account sign-in is provided through Supabase Auth. You may sign in with email and password, with an email magic link, or with an OAuth identity provider (such as Google). When you use an OAuth provider, we receive only the limited profile fields you authorize that provider to share with us (typically your name, email address, and profile picture).

Cross-site single sign-on between Kalopaideia and The Reading Mansion is implemented as a signed token scoped to the newcharterventures.com domain. Signing out on either site invalidates the token on both sites.

6. Reading Analytics

For signed-in users, where you have consented (in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland) or have not opted out (elsewhere), we record a lightweight summary of your reading activity on Kalopaideia. The recorded events are:

  • session start and end times;
  • the language or library text being viewed;
  • primer panes opened;
  • audio plays;
  • pronunciation tooltips opened;
  • library lines scrolled past;
  • daily-word card views;
  • device class (desktop / mobile / tablet); and
  • user-agent string.

This data powers your personal Reading Analytics dashboard at newcharterventures.com/paideia/analytics and, in anonymized aggregate, helps us understand which texts and features are most useful to readers. We do not record keystrokes, cursor movements, page screenshots, or any content you read elsewhere. We never share or sell reading-analytics data with third parties.

Consent model.

  • Users in the United States and elsewhere outside the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland. Reading analytics are enabled by default for signed-in users. You may opt out at any time from your dashboard (toggle: "Track my reading on Kalopaideia") or by sending a DNT: 1 request header.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland. Reading analytics are opt-in. We will not record reading-analytics events for your account unless and until you affirmatively enable the dashboard. You may withdraw consent at any time from the same dashboard control, with no effect on the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal.

Once you have opted out (or, in the EEA/UK/CH, declined to opt in), no new events are accepted from your account. You may also download or delete all of your reading-analytics data from the dashboard at any time. Raw event records are retained for up to three hundred sixty-five (365) days; daily roll-ups may be retained indefinitely in anonymized aggregated form.

8. Your Rights

The rights described in this Section depend on the law of your jurisdiction. Where a right is granted by law, we will honor it without discrimination against you for exercising it. To exercise any right, write to info@newcharterventures.com with a subject line that identifies your request (e.g., "Access Request," "Erasure Request," "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"). We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law, generally within thirty (30) to forty-five (45) days.

8.1 California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1798.100 et seq.) to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and retain;
  • access and obtain a copy of that information;
  • delete that information, subject to statutory exceptions;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising;
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information; and
  • be free from discrimination for exercising any CCPA/CPRA right.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under CCPA/CPRA, nor do we use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the Service. You may nevertheless exercise the right to opt out by writing to info@newcharterventures.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information."

Authorized agents. You may designate an authorized agent to submit a CCPA/CPRA request on your behalf. We will require written authorization signed by you and may require verification of your identity directly before responding.

We respond to verified consumer requests within forty-five (45) days, extendable by an additional forty-five (45) days where reasonably necessary, with notice to you.

8.2 EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the data controller is New Charter Ventures LLC, contactable at info@newcharterventures.com. The lawful bases on which we process your personal data are described in Section 2.1.

You have rights under the GDPR and corresponding national laws to:

  • access your personal data and obtain a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • erase your personal data, subject to statutory exceptions;
  • restrict processing of your personal data;
  • object to processing (including for direct-marketing purposes);
  • port your personal data to another controller;
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing; and
  • not be subject to solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects (see Section 8.4).

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@newcharterventures.com with the relevant subject line and include the email address associated with your account. We will respond within thirty (30) days.

Supervisory authority. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority in your country of residence, place of work, or place of an alleged GDPR infringement.

8.3 Other jurisdictions

Residents of jurisdictions that grant additional privacy rights (including, without limitation, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, and emerging state-level laws in the United States such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah) may have analogous rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability. Write to info@newcharterventures.com and we will honor your rights to the extent required by applicable law.

8.4 Automated decision-making and scoring

We do not use automated processing to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Some educational features of the Service — including checkpoint quizzes, capstone exams, and Diploma issuance — involve automated scoring against published rubrics, and AI-assisted models may participate in that scoring. The Diploma is an honorary certificate of completion (not an accredited credential), and its issuance does not produce a legal or similarly significant effect within the meaning of GDPR Article 22 or analogous laws.

If you believe an automated score, a Diploma issuance record, or any other automatically produced output is erroneous, you may contact us at info@newcharterventures.com for human review. Where required by applicable law, we will provide a meaningful explanation of the scoring logic involved, an opportunity to express your point of view, and a route to a manual re-review.
