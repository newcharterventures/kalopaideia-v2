Kalopaideia teaches Greek the way it was taught for two thousand years before the textbook era: by ear, by recitation, by reading. We begin with the sounds, then with short authentic phrases, then with whole sentences from Xenophon, Plato, and the New Testament. Grammar is introduced when the form has already been met in living text. Every stage ends with reading. The student who finishes the course earns the Kalopaideia Diploma in Classical Greek, recorded on the Base blockchain in perpetuity.

Stage V · The Capstone

The Author

Sustained reading of a major author. Choose your track. Sit the examination. Earn the diploma.

Track I

The Philosopher

Plato, Crito

The most accessible Plato. A short dialogue with a definite shape, the Socratic style, the philosophical vocabulary.

Track II

The Historian

Xenophon, Anabasis Bk I

The classic intermediate reader. Simple syntax, vivid narrative.

Track III

The Evangelist

Gospel of Mark, Koine

The shortest, earliest Gospel. The bridge from Attic into the New Testament.
