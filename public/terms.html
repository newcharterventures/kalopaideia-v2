1. About Kalopaideia

Kalopaideia is an educational and cultural reference publication. Each day, the Site presents one word from each of several classical and historical languages — together with its pronunciation, inflection, etymology, literary context, and a short cultural vignette — and a daily reading from the literary corpus of each language. The Site additionally publishes a long-form audio library of canonical works (the "Akousma") and a structured language curriculum (the "Curriculum") with auto-graded assessments and an honorary certificate of completion (the "Diploma").

The Site is published as a work of literary scholarship and cultural commentary. It is not a credentialed academic institution, a degree-granting program, a professional advisory service, or a substitute for accredited classical-languages instruction. The Diploma is not accredited by any educational authority and confers no academic credit, degree, or professional qualification of any kind. Statements to the contrary, whether on the Site or elsewhere, are unauthorized.
