Featured Sponsor

THE HELLENIC SOCIETY OF AMERICA

Preserving the living tradition of classical Greek thought

Established in 1881, the Hellenic Society of America is a fellowship dedicated to the study and appreciation of ancient Greece. We bring together distinguished classicists, academics, students, and passionate amateurs in a shared commitment to exploring the profound legacy of Hellenic civilization and its enduring relevance in the modern world.

Members receive our distinguished quarterly journal, "The Hellenic Review," featuring scholarly articles, book reviews, and translations that illuminate diverse aspects of Greek history, philosophy, literature, and art.

The society hosts regular symposia, lectures, and cultural events, fostering a vibrant intellectual community across the nation. Join us in celebrating the timeless wisdom and artistic achievements that continue to shape our cultural heritage. Embrace the opportunity to deepen your understanding and connection to the classical world through our collective endeavors.
