Kalopaideia teaches Greek the way it was taught for two thousand years before the textbook era: by ear, by recitation, by reading. We do not begin with grammar tables. We begin with the sounds, then short authentic phrases, then whole sentences from Xenophon, Plato, and the New Testament. Grammar is introduced when the form has already been met in living text. Every stage ends with reading. The student who finishes the course earns the Kalopaideia Diploma in Classical Greek, recorded on the Base blockchain in perpetuity.
