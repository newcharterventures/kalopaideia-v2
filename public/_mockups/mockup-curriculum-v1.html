The Three Tracks
Track I
The Philosopher
Plato, Crito (complete)
The most accessible Plato. A complete short dialogue with a definite shape. Introduces the Socratic style and the major philosophical vocabulary.
Track II
The Historian
Xenophon, Anabasis Book I
The classic intermediate reader. Simple syntax, vivid narrative. The book on which generations of Greek students cut their teeth.
Track III
The Evangelist
The Gospel of Mark (Koine)
The shortest and earliest Gospel. Koine, not Attic, but the bridge is short. Opens the entire New Testament to the student.
