The Greek Course

A five-room atrium, walked stage by stage. From the alphabet to Plato.

Kalopaideia teaches Greek the way it was taught for two thousand years before the textbook era: by ear, by recitation, by reading. We do not begin with grammar tables. We begin with the sounds, then short authentic phrases, then whole sentences from Xenophon, Plato, and the New Testament. Grammar enters when the form has already been met in living text. Twelve to eighteen months at an hour a day.
