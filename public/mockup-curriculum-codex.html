The Greek Course

From the alphabet to Plato, in five guided stages

❦ ❦ ❦

Kalopaideia teaches Greek the way it was taught for two thousand years before the textbook era: by ear, by recitation, by reading. We do not begin with grammar tables. We begin with the sounds, then with short authentic phrases, then with whole sentences from Xenophon, Plato, and the New Testament. Grammar is introduced when the student has already met the form in living text. Every stage ends with reading. The course runs twelve to eighteen months at an hour a day. The student who finishes it earns the Kalopaideia Diploma in Classical Greek, recorded irrevocably on the Base blockchain.

Stage V

The Author

12–24 weeks · capstone

Sustained reading of a major author. Choose your track. Sit the examination. Earn the diploma.

Track I
The Philosopher
Plato, Crito (complete dialogue, c. 6,200 words)
The most accessible Plato. A short dialogue with a definite shape; the Socratic style; the major philosophical vocabulary.
Track II
The Historian
Xenophon, Anabasis Book I (c. 11,000 words)
Simple syntax, vivid narrative. The book on which generations of Greek students cut their teeth.
Track III
The Evangelist
The Gospel of Mark, in Koine (c. 11,300 words)
The shortest and earliest Gospel. The bridge between Attic and the rest of the New Testament.
