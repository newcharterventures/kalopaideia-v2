# German Curriculum — Full Content Delivery

**Language:** Classical German  
**Philosophy:** Reading-led. From the alphabet to Kafka.  
**Total lessons:** 49 (8 Stage 1 + 12 Stage 2 + 16 Stage 3 + 13 Stage 4)  
**Track guides:** 3 (Poet, Philosopher, Novelist)  
**Capstone exam:** 1 (4 sections, 100 points, 70% pass / 85% honors)

## Structure

### Stage 1: The Sound and the Shape (8 lessons)
Lessons 1.1–1.8: alphabet, umlauts, ß, ch sounds, final devoicing, vowel length, reading the names (Goethe, Nietzsche, etc.), first sentences.

### Stage 2: The Sentence (12 lessons)
Lessons 2.1–2.12: three genders, definite/indefinite articles, four cases (nominative, accusative, dative, genitive), present tense (regular + sein/haben/werden), V2 word order, adjectives/adverbs, first Heine poem.

### Stage 3: The Tenses and the Cases (16 lessons)
Lessons 3.1–3.16: weak/strong/mixed nouns, adjective declension (strong/weak/mixed), accusative/dative/two-way prepositions, present perfect, Präteritum, Plusquamperfekt, separable verbs, modal verbs, readings (Goethe "Heidenröslein," Schiller "Der Handschuh").

### Stage 4: The Subjunctive and the Subordinate (13 lessons)
Lessons 4.1–4.13: Konjunktiv I & II (present + perfect), conditional sentences, würde + infinitive, relative clauses, infinitive constructions (zu), passive voice (all tenses + modals), readings (Heine "Die Lorelei," Goethe "Erlkönig").

### Stage 5: The Author (3 tracks)
- **Poet:** Goethe, West-östlicher Divan (15 poems ~2K words) + Heidenröslein, Erlkönig, Der Zauberlehrling (full).
- **Philosopher:** Nietzsche, Also sprach Zarathustra Vorrede §1–10 (~8K words).
- **Novelist:** Kafka, Die Verwandlung (entire, ~22K words).

## Capstone Examination

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Sections:**
- **Part A:** Sight translation (no notes). 6 items, track-specific. Goethe quatrains (Poet), Zarathustra opening (Philosopher), Kafka opening sentence (Novelist).
- **Part B:** Parsing. 5 items. Verb forms, noun forms, adjective declension.
- **Part C:** Composition (English → German). 5 items. Simple to complex sentences, passive voice, conditionals.
- **Part D:** Multiple choice. 8 items. Vocabulary, idiom, case/auxiliary usage.

**Scoring:** 100 points. Pass = 70%, Honors = 85%.

**Certification:** On-chain ERC-721 soulbound token. Permanent, irrevocable. Recorded on Ethereum mainnet.

## Voice Rules (Anti-Slop)

Every prose block, reading note, and exercise instruction follows the SOUL.md anti-slop rules:
- NO -ly adverbs.
- NO em dashes.
- NO three-item lists (two or one).
- NO filler phrases ("at its core," "it's worth noting," etc.).
- NO false agency.
- Declarative, scholarly, warm. Real German examples from Goethe, Schiller, Hölderlin, Heine, Rilke, Nietzsche, Kafka, Mann in EVERY lesson.

## Lesson Shape

Every lesson follows the gold standard of `greek/lesson-1.1.json`:
- **Prose opener** with real German examples.
- **Letter grids** (Stage 1) or **paradigm tables** (Stages 2-4) with IPA, audio paths, notes.
- **Self-check** section with 5-10 items.
- **Reading preview** with German + transliteration/gloss + audio.
